# Original pt-MZ guidance and editor samples

25 October 2026. Four public guides at /orientacoes: first CV, tailoring to an opportunity, application email, pre-send review. The email is copied locally, never sent by CVMais. The six-item checklist is memory-only, resets on leaving the page and collects no new personal data.

Editor sample library: role, profile, individual experience/education descriptions, skills and languages. Twelve industry groups, three career stages, and optional grammatical feminine/masculine/neutral wording. These are editorial coverage choices, not a statistically ranked list of popular professions in Mozambique. Gender does not determine skills, seniority or recommended occupations; no gender field is added to the CV/database.

Samples are original Portuguese text. Users preview before insertion. Nonempty fields require explicit replacement confirmation or allow append; insertion can be undone while that field remains unchanged. Existing names, photos, employers, dates, qualifications, other fields and other entries are preserved. Experience and education samples do not auto-create employers, institutions or dates. Bracketed placeholders require adaptation; even ready-to-use task/skill examples must reflect the person's actual experience. No invented numeric achievements, certificates, years of experience or hiring guarantees.

General editorial references (international, not Mozambique hiring requirements):
- https://europass.europa.eu/en/create-europass-cv — relevance to the vacancy, clear evidence and factual content.
- https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/cv-sections — job-specific content, real projects/volunteering for first employment.
- https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/job-profiles/admin-assistant — examples of administrative tasks.
- https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/job-profiles/sales-assistant — examples of customer-facing retail tasks.
- Each guide links its supplementary reading; examples are labelled illustrative.

No source text, third-party template layout or private CV was copied. This library does not use an AI API, send field contents to an external service or change storage/authentication. Homepage preview is an original responsive HTML/CSS document with an existing illustrative portrait, two columns, clear sections, fictional work/education and an explicit fictional-data footer.
