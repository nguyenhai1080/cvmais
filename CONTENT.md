# Original pt-MZ guidance and editor samples

25 October 2026. Five public guides at /orientacoes: first CV, tailoring to an opportunity, application email, pre-send review and interview preparation. All five are linked from the homepage and have editor CTAs. The email is copied locally, never sent by CVMais. The six-item checklist is memory-only, resets on leaving the page and collects no new personal data.

Editor sample library: role, profile, individual experience/education descriptions, skills and languages. Twelve industry groups, three career stages, and optional grammatical feminine/masculine/neutral wording. These are editorial coverage choices, not a statistically ranked list of popular professions in Mozambique. Gender does not determine skills, seniority or recommended occupations; no gender field is added to the CV/database.

Samples are original Portuguese text. Users preview before insertion. Nonempty fields require explicit replacement confirmation or allow append; insertion can be undone while that field remains unchanged. Existing names, photos, employers, dates, qualifications, other fields and other entries are preserved. Experience and education samples do not auto-create employers, institutions or dates. Bracketed placeholders require adaptation; even ready-to-use task/skill examples must reflect the person's actual experience. No invented numeric achievements, certificates, years of experience or hiring guarantees.

General editorial references (international, not Mozambique hiring requirements):
- https://europass.europa.eu/en/create-europass-cv — relevance to the vacancy, clear evidence and factual content.
- https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/cv-sections — job-specific content, real projects/volunteering for first employment.
- https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/job-profiles/admin-assistant — examples of administrative tasks.
- https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/job-profiles/sales-assistant — examples of customer-facing retail tasks.
- Each guide links its supplementary reading; examples are labelled illustrative.

No source text, third-party template layout or private CV was copied. This library does not use an AI API, send field contents to an external service or change storage/authentication. Homepage preview is an original responsive HTML/CSS document with an existing illustrative portrait, two columns, clear sections, fictional work/education and an explicit fictional-data footer.


## Interview preparation — 25 October 2026

Original pt-MZ guide /orientacoes/preparar-entrevista, fully translated for the optional English interface. Covers preparation, a first-job introduction, STAR evidence, honest handling of difficult/technical questions, listening and clarification, online setup with a connection/power backup plan, questions to the organisation and respectful follow-up. Examples are labelled fictional/illustrative. No hiring guarantee or unsupported outcome metrics.

The training panel has four STAR note fields, a practice-question selector, explicit clipboard copying and five temporary preparation checks. State remains in page memory, is not sent to the server and resets on leaving/reload. Language changes preserve authored notes verbatim. No microphone, camera, recording, external analysis or new DB fields are introduced.

Primary research: https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/interview-advice/ ; https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/interview-advice/the-star-method ; https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/how-to-do-well-in-video-interviews . International general references were adapted into original wording, without importing UK eligibility rules or copying source examples. Mozambican native-speaker/physical-device pilot remains pending.

## Industry letters — 27 October 2026

Original PT-MZ/English letter scaffolds for twelve fields and two background levels, with three original editorial tips per field in both UI languages. Sixth guide: /orientacoes/cartas-por-area. Independent letter language defaults to Portuguese; interface language changes do not translate user-edited drafts. Examples explicitly require replacing bracketed details and removing untrue claims. Drafts stay in page memory and disappear after leaving/reload; copy is explicit, no sending or external API. Changes to field/background/example language only affect the sample; a working draft is replaced only after explicit confirmation. Follow the advert's instructions and protect confidential third-party data.

Primary reference for general letter structure and tailoring: https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/covering-letter . Industry examples/tips are original editorial guidance, not copied sample letters or locally regulated eligibility advice. No hiring guarantees. Native PT-MZ review remains pending.
